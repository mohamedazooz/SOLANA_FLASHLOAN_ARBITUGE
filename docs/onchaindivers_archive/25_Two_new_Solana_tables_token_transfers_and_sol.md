- **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

<p>We added two new chain-wide Solana tables to OnChainDivers:</p><ul><li><p><code>token_transfers</code> records token movement between wallets;</p></li><li><p><code>sol_top_ups</code> records direct SOL funding between wallets.</p></li></ul><p>The point is not another precomputed metric. It uses the last 24 complete UTC hours and does not create a separate data copy.</p><div class="highlighted_code_block" data-attrs="{&quot;language&quot;:&quot;sql&quot;,&quot;nodeId&quot;:&quot;38581077-31f1-427e-8a69-441677a64ab7&quot;}" data-component-name="HighlightedCodeBlockToDOM"><pre class="shiki"><code class="language-sql">WITH
    toStartOfHour(now()) AS end_hour,
    end_hour - INTERVAL 24 HOUR AS start_hour
SELECT
    hour,
    sum(token_transfers_count) AS token_transfers_count,
    sum(sol_top_ups_count) AS sol_top_ups_count
FROM
(
    SELECT
        toStartOfHour(block_time) AS hour,
        count() AS token_transfers_count,
        toUInt64(0) AS sol_top_ups_count
    FROM token_transfers
    WHERE block_time &gt;= start_hour
      AND block_time &lt; end_hour
    GROUP BY hour

    UNION ALL

    SELECT
        toStartOfHour(block_time) AS hour,
        toUInt64(0) AS token_transfers_count,
        count() AS sol_top_ups_count
    FROM sol_top_ups
    WHERE block_time &gt;= start_hour
      AND block_time &lt; end_hour
    GROUP BY hour
)
GROUP BY hour
ORDER BY hour;</code></pre></div><div class="captioned-image-container"><figure><a class="image-link image2 is-viewable-img" target="_blank" The tables open several broader research directions.</p><h3>Funding-to-action timelines</h3><p>Join <code>sol_top_ups</code> to swaps, token creation, staking, governance or program interactions. Measure how funding arrives before execution and whether the same sources recur.</p><h3>Transfer topology</h3><p>Compare unique senders with receivers. One-to-many fan-out can represent distribution; many-to-one fan-in can represent consolidation. Equal transfer counts can describe opposite behavior.</p><h3>Launcher and wallet fingerprints</h3><p>Combine top-up size, funding lead time, fee payer, transfer batch sizes and destination-set overlap. Repeated sequences can remain recognizable even when token mints change.</p><h3>Holder-quality research</h3><p>A token sent to 1,000 addresses is not meaningfully distributed if most supply remains under common control or later converges on the same collector. Raw transfers make that test possible.</p><h3>Recurring ecosystem pulses</h3><p>The hourly chart can become a regular Solana pulse: new active mints, dominant transfer programs, top-up bursts, unusual funding corridors and changes in sender/receiver breadth.</p><h2>How a trader can use this without treating it as a signal</h2><p>These tables are best used as market-quality filters.</p><p>Before treating rapid holder growth as demand, check whether transfers came from many independent sources or one distributor. Before treating an unchanged chart as price stability, check whether trading continued after the launch minute. Before treating a funded buyer as suspicious, check whether the funding source, amount and timing recur across other launches.</p><p>None of those checks gives a mechanical entry or exit. They reduce false positives and help distinguish a market from a scripted sequence of transactions.</p><h2>Important boundaries</h2><ul><li><p>Addresses are not automatically people or independent entities.</p></li><li><p>Transfer-row counts are not token value.</p></li><li><p>Top-ups show funding, not intent.</p></li><li><p>Same-second event ordering requires finer instruction-level keys.</p></li><li><p>Price outcomes should be censored when no post-launch market forms.</p></li></ul><h2>Where we go next</h2><p>The next research pass will move from counts to sequences:</p><ul><li><p>destination-wallet overlap across launches;</p></li><li><p>supply-weighted distribution rather than address count;</p></li><li><p>later consolidation into common collectors;</p></li><li><p>repeated funders, fee payers and transfer sources;</p></li><li><p>matched price and liquidity outcomes only for tokens with continued trading.</p></li></ul><p><em>OnChainDivers built </em><code>token_transfers</code><em> and </em><code>sol_top_ups</code><em> as chain-wide primitives.
