# Memecoins: Pump or Dump? Our Kaggle competition with a $5,000 prize pool is live

- **Date**: 2026-10-08
- **Original URL**: [https://onchaindivers.substack.com/p/memecoins-pump-or-dump-our-kaggle](https://onchaindivers.substack.com/p/memecoins-pump-or-dump-our-kaggle)
- **Subtitle**: Real pump.fun data, a simple trading task, and full support from our team. Open until December 1, 2026.
- **Description**: Real pump.fun data, a simple trading task, and full support from our team. Open until December 1, 2026.

---

<p>We’ve launched a Kaggle competition with a $5,000 prize pool, and it’s already moving: <strong>852 submissions so far</strong>, a daily ranking, and people already discussing it in our chat.<br><br><strong>Join and read more here:</strong> <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/memecoins-pump-or-dump/overview">https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/memecoins-pump-or-dump/overview</a></p><p>The competition runs until December 1, 2026.</p><div><hr></div><h4>What is Kaggle?</h4><p>It’s a platform where machine learning competitions are held and where the latest approaches and models are tested in conditions that are very close to real-world problems.<br>In short, Kaggle is widely considered one of the best ways to learn machine learning fast through practice.<br>And it’s also a lot of fun.</p><h4>What do you need to do?</h4><p>For every memecoin on <strong>Pump.fun</strong>, you get access to:<br>• all mint data<br>• all Pump.fun trades<br>• migration data<br>• all PumpSwap trades from the first minute after migration<br>Based on this data, you either skip the token or buy it and set a take-profit target for the next hour.<br>After that, either the price hits your take-profit target, or the position is closed at the end of the hour after your decision.<br>Your final result is your total PnL across all tokens.</p><h4>What about looking into the future?</h4><p>The final score will be calculated on data that was not available at the time of your final model submission.<br>So there is <strong>no way</strong> to look into the future, accidentally use future data, or otherwise leak the answers.</p><h4>How can you join?</h4><p>You can run your code directly on Kaggle.<br>You can create a Kaggle API key and give Claude or ChatGPT the competition link.<br>Or you can use our Telegram bot and simply send your submission file there.<br>All options work — use whatever is most convenient for you.</p><h4>Who is this competition for?</h4><p><strong>For crypto people</strong> - this is a great opportunity to get into machine learning using real blockchain data.<br><strong>For machine learning specialists</strong> - it’s a great way to get into blockchain, on-chain data, and algorithmic trading.<br>And in <strong>both cases</strong>, you’ll be working on a fairly advanced real-world problem with our full support 24/7.</p><h4><br><strong>And most importantly — ask questions. ANY questions.</strong></h4><p><strong>Our chat:</strong> <a href="https://t.co/w56zCI7jOw">https://t.me/BlockchainExploratorsChat</a><br><strong>DM:</strong> <a href="https://t.co/L2SiBWRUXm">https://t.me/ParrotExplorator</a></p><p>The source code for all solutions will be published after the deadline, and everything will be made available to the community.<br><br><strong>As soon as something is unclear or you’re not sure about something — just ask.<br>Don’t understand the data — ask.<br>Don’t understand how Pump.fun works — ask.<br>Don’t know where to start with ML — ask.<br>Have a question about submissions, the API, the metric, or the rules — ask.</strong><br>⠀<br>This competition was made for you and to make participation as convenient
