Pump.fun derives the child’s starting virtual quote reserves from the quote coin’s live curve or pool reserves, targeting roughly the same raise as a normal launch but denominating it in the parent coin.[1]</p><p>There is one important limit: <strong>the graph is currently only one level deep.</strong> If coin B is quoted in coin A, B cannot itself become the quote for coin C. Pump.fun rejects that path with CurveDepthExceeded.[1]</p><p>Trading is handled by a matching route. PumpSwap’s multi_hop_swap moves through two or more pools and curves atomically, keeps the intermediate coin out of the user’s wallet, applies one final slippage check, and emits one event per hop.[2] The official sell example is exactly the shape seen on-chain:</p><p>child coin → Pump quote coin → SOL</p><p>multi_hop_curve_swap is the Pump-program side of that route. The official docs say it is callable only by PumpSwap’s signed global-config PDA; integrators are expected to call PumpSwap’s outer multi_hop_swap, not invoke the curve method directly.[2]</p><p>This is different from the earlier quote-token expansion. Pump.fun V2 already supported configured quote assets such as PUMP, USDC, xStocks and other listed tokens. The October update makes a much larger set dynamic: a Pump.fun coin can now become the reserve currency for another Pump.fun coin.[1][3]</p><h3>How H1B’s migration threshold is calculated</h3><p>A token-paired curve does not keep an 85 SOL migration threshold. Deduplicate creation rows to one row per mint, then join the child’s quote_coin to another creation’s mint.</p><div class="highlighted_code_block" data-attrs="{&quot;language&quot;:&quot;sql&quot;,&quot;nodeId&quot;:&quot;8bd1a945-8873-4e2e-9a70-f42a01db69f3&quot;}" data-component-name="HighlightedCodeBlockToDOM"><pre class="shiki"><code class="language-sql">WITH
    toDateTime('2026-10-01 00:00:00', 'UTC') AS start_ts,
    toDateTime('2026-10-08 20:30:00', 'UTC') AS end_ts,
    creations AS
    (
        SELECT
            replaceAll(toString(mint), char(0), '') AS mint,
            argMin(replaceAll(toString(name), char(0), ''),
                   tuple(block_time, slot, tx_idx)) AS name,
            argMin(replaceAll(toString(symbol), char(0), ''),
                   tuple(block_time, slot, tx_idx)) AS symbol,
            argMin(replaceAll(toString(creator), char(0), ''),
                   tuple(block_time, slot, tx_idx)) AS creator,
            argMin(quote_coin,
                   tuple(block_time, slot, tx_idx)) AS quote_coin,
            min(block_time) AS created_at,
            argMin(signature,
                   tuple(block_time, slot, tx_idx)) AS creation_signature
        FROM default.pumpfun_token_creation
        WHERE block_time &gt;= toDateTime('2024-01-01 00:00:00', 'UTC')
          AND block_time &lt; end_ts
        GROUP BY mint
    )
SELECT
    child.created_at,
    child.mint AS child_mint,
    child.name AS child_name,
    child.symbol AS child_symbol,
    parent.mint AS quote_mint,
    parent.name AS quote_name,
    parent.symbol AS quote_symbol,
    child.creator = parent.creator AS same_creator
FROM creations AS child
INNER JOIN creations AS parent
    ON child.quote_coin = parent.mint
WHERE child.created_at &gt;= start_ts
  AND child.created_at &lt; end_ts
  AND parent.created_at &lt;= child.created_at
ORDER BY child.created_at DESC;</code></pre></div><p>The historical side of the CTE has an explicit lower bound because the parent coin may predate the child window by months. The final condition prevents a future or reprocessed parent row from being attached to an earlier child. Deduplication before the join is essential: joining raw creation rows can multiply one child when historical rows have been reprocessed.</p><h3>4,020 children and a launch graph that went vertical</h3><p>For the half-open window 2026-10-01 00:00:00 through 2026-10-08 20:30:00 UTC, the join returned:</p><ul><li><p><strong>4,020</strong> child launches quoted in another Pump.fun-created coin;</p></li><li><p><strong>239</strong> distinct quote-parent mints;</p></li><li><p><strong>2,094</strong> distinct child creators;</p></li><li><p>only <strong>88 launches, or 2.19%</strong>, where child and quote parent had the same creator;</p></li><li><p>a median quote-parent age of <strong>460.29 hours</strong>, about 19.2 days;</p></li><li><p><strong>717</strong> children whose quote parent was less than one hour old;</p></li><li><p><strong>1,010</strong> whose quote parent was less than one day old.</p></li></ul><p>The daily count sat between 322 and 453 from October 1 through October 7. Then October 8 produced <strong>1,376 launches by 20:30 UTC</strong>, despite being a partial day. The 19:00–20:00 UTC bucket alone contained <strong>695</strong> coin-on-coin launches.</p><p>The launches traded. Among 3,383 with a complete one-hour observation window, <strong>2,770 (81.88%)</strong> had at least one successful transaction already indexed in pumpfun_v2_swaps. The median across the completed cohort was four indexed transactions; 275 launches reached at least 100 and 32 reached at least 1,000. Treat those as lower bounds until the decoder covers the new multi-hop instruction. The supplied H1B sell is one known omission.</p><h3>The best child/parent combinations</h3><p>The self-join produces markets that read more like punchlines than trading pairs:</p><div For every child coin, a data product now needs:</p><ol><li><p>the child’s quote mint from creation;</p></li><li><p>the quote coin’s own SOL, USDC, or listed-token market;</p></li><li><p>both token decimal domains;</p></li><li><p>the route and venue used by each hop;</p></li><li><p>event coverage for both multi_hop_swap and multi_hop_curve_swap.</p></li></ol><p>A child price is first expressed in units of its parent coin. A dollar or SOL price is a composed price:</p><p>child / parent × parent / SOL × SOL / USD</p><p>The same rule applies to turnover and creator fees. Raw quote amounts cannot be summed across parent mints, and a parent coin’s volatility becomes part of the child’s price even when the child’s own curve does not move.</p><p>The event model also matters. Pump.fun documents one event per hop, while the intermediate parent coin nets to zero for the user.[2] Indexing only wallet deltas will see H1B leave and SOL arrive but can miss the real LAYOFF volume in the middle. Indexing only familiar trade discriminators can miss the entire route.</p><h3>The correction</h3><p>We missed the supplied transaction in the current historical slice. The live decoder already emits multi_hop_curve_swap rows for the same H1B/LAYOFF curve, so no new instruction or route-shape implementation is required. The remaining task is to let the backfill reach this signature and verify the reconstructed rows.</p><p>The creation layer was already enough to enumerate the new market graph. The raw transaction and Pump.fun’s updated docs identify the expected rows precisely. The indexer must decode every emitted hop event and preserve the child and parent mints rather than flattening the route into a synthetic H1B/SOL trade. For the supplied signature, that means a Pump curve leg for H1B/LAYOFF plus the connected PumpSwap leg for LAYOFF/WSOL.</p><p><strong>Pump.fun did not merely add another quote token. It made quote selection recursive across its own launch graph—one level deep for now—and gave the ecosystem an atomic router to trade through it.</strong></p><h3>Method and
