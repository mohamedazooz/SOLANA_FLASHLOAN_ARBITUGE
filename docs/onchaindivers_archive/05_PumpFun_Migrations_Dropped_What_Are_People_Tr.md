Priced volume did not: it rebounded in the final week even as migrations declined again. This is why “fewer migrations,” “less PumpSwap volume,” and “more trading in older tokens” should not be used interchangeably.</p><p>Across the endpoints, migrations declined <strong>21.5%</strong>, while priced PumpFun-token volume declined <strong>30.6%</strong>. Yet the latest week’s volume was <strong>7.7% higher</strong> than the week before. The supply of new pools matters, but turnover remains episodic and concentrated in whichever fresh tokens capture attention.</p><h2>What counts as a migration?</h2><p>We did not use the first PumpSwap trade as a proxy. A migration is a decoded row in <code>pfamm_migrations</code>, and the daily series counts unique migrated mints. Pump’s official documentation says canonical PumpSwap pools created through the Pump program’s migration instruction use pool index zero.[1]</p><p>That distinction matters more after synthetic migration. Pump’s current design allows the buy that completes a bonding curve to consume tokens against the future pool’s implied reserves before the pool exists; <code>migrate_v2</code> subsequently creates the actual pool at the resulting reserve state.[2] A completing trade and the pool-creation event are therefore related, but they are not the same timestamp.</p><h2>Reproduce the histogram</h2><p>The publication query uses fixed UTC windows and keeps the unmatched bucket in the denominator. The full executable query is in <code>sql/05_volume_by_token_age_week_optimized.sql</code>; its core classification is:</p><div class="highlighted_code_block" data-attrs="{&quot;language&quot;:&quot;plaintext&quot;,&quot;nodeId&quot;:&quot;1fa95ce9-0d33-4985-a8bb-3afecd79aac7&quot;}" data-component-name="HighlightedCodeBlockToDOM"><pre class="shiki"><code class="language-plaintext">multiIf(
    created_at IS NULL, 'unknown / non-PumpFun',
    block_time &lt; created_at, 'pre-creation anomaly',
    dateDiff('second', created_at, block_time) &lt; 3600, '&lt;1 hour',
    dateDiff('second', created_at, block_time) &lt; 21600, '1–6 hours',
    dateDiff('second', created_at, block_time) &lt; 86400, '6–24 hours',
    dateDiff('second', created_at, block_time) &lt; 259200, '1–3 days',
    dateDiff('second', created_at, block_time) &lt; 604800, '3–7 days',
    dateDiff('second', created_at, block_time) &lt; 2592000, '7–30 days',
    dateDiff('second', created_at, block_time) &lt; 7776000, '30–90 days',
    dateDiff('second', created_at, block_time) &lt; 31536000, '90–365 days',
    '365+ days'
) AS age_bucket</code></pre></div><p>USD volume is calculated from raw quote amount and the indexer’s daily quote-price series:</p><div class="highlighted_code_block" data-attrs="{&quot;language&quot;:&quot;plaintext&quot;,&quot;nodeId&quot;:&quot;f610f0a5-8667-4cf4-b328-20717e5fa365&quot;}" data-component-name="HighlightedCodeBlockToDOM"><pre class="shiki"><code class="language-plaintext">toFloat64(quote_token_amount) / 1000000.0 * quote_price_usd</code></pre></div><p>For WSOL, the quote-price series is expressed per one million raw quote units. A ten-trade validation sample confirmed that this matches the enriched view exactly; treating <code>quote_price_usd</code> as the price of one whole SOL would introduce a 1,000× error.</p><p>The charts and summary tables can be regenerated with: python3 analyze_and_plot.py</p><h2>Evidence boundary</h2><p>This histogram describes <strong>priced indexed volume</strong>, not every PumpSwap row.</p><ul><li><p>Daily quote prices covered <strong>62.9% of indexed swap rows</strong> in the latest week. Most uncovered rows involved custom Pump-coin quote assets. Their rows remain in the QA denominator, but they cannot contribute to USD-volume shares without a defensible historical quote price. The chart may therefore overweight SOL-quoted activity.</p></li><li><p>“Unknown/non-PumpFun” means the base mint did not match <code>pumpfun_token_creation</code>; it does not prove that the asset has no relationship to Pump’s ecosystem.</p></li><li><p>Addresses and signatures are not users. The article reports volume and migration events, not unique people.</p></li><li><p>An exact duplicate audit of <strong>734,300 rows</strong> in a fixed one-hour sample found <strong>zero duplicate event keys</strong>. Weekly approximate-cardinality checks were within roughly 0.3% of raw rows, consistent with estimator error rather than material duplication.</p></li><li><p>The latest day is October 26. October 27 is the exclusive cutoff, so no partial day is mixed into the weekly comparisons.</p></li></ul><h2>Takeaway</h2><p>PumpFun migrations are down from their late-summer peak, and the latest complete week contained 21.5% fewer migrations than the first week of this four-week comparison. PumpSwap volume also finished lower—but traders did not broadly retreat into old pools.</p><p>The market is still built around immediacy. Almost half of priced PumpFun-token volume occurred before a token was one hour old, and nearly three quarters before it was one day old. Older pools gained a little share, but the center of gravity remains the launch window.</p><h2>Sources</h2><p>[1] https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/639b87fb45c3adeffa6f7c4a1bf0cb85e392bf22/docs/PUMP_SWAP_README.md — PumpSwap (Pump AMM) program [2] https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/639b87fb45c3adeffa6f7c4a1bf0cb85e392bf22/docs/SYNTHETIC_MIGRATION.md — Synthetic Migration</p><div><hr></div><p><em>OnChain Divers provides decoded Solana data: launch, swap and migration data for researchers building lifecycle-aware market models.
