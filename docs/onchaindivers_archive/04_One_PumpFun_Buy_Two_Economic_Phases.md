    signature,
    ix_idx,
    sum(base_coin_amount)  AS total_base_out,
    sum(quote_coin_amount) AS total_quote_into_reserves

WHERE signature = '3QWeY371w8Rs4ZPBB1f4MeEgGt963PNvKWKTD3fMiphkonyYicWd6tLFJ14KkXbsKqdDeuLtPNSnzzdJQd59h6Gr'

    is_post_trade = 0, 'bonding-curve virtual reserves after',

    'unknown'

provided_gas_limit
consumed_gas
fee
signing_wallet
fee_payer
parent_program</code></pre></div><p>Collapse them with <code>any</code>, <code>argMin</code>, or <code>max</code> only after confirming within-instruction consistency.</p><h2>A safe transaction-level model</h2><p>For analytics, preserve both grains:</p><div class="highlighted_code_block" data-attrs="{&quot;language&quot;:&quot;plaintext&quot;,&quot;nodeId&quot;:&quot;085d587b-80c5-486f-9537-48fda11b31a5&quot;}" data-component-name="HighlightedCodeBlockToDOM"><pre class="shiki"><code class="language-plaintext">instruction fact:
(signature, ix_idx)

leg fact:
(signature, ix_idx, is_post_trade)</code></pre></div><p>The instruction fact answers:</p><ul><li><p>how many buys occurred;</p></li><li><p>how many wallets traded;</p></li><li><p>total tokens received;</p></li><li><p>total quote committed to reserves.</p></li></ul><p>The leg fact answers:</p><ul><li><p>how much completed the curve;</p></li><li><p>how much traded against the future pool;</p></li><li><p>which reserve state belongs to each phase.</p></li></ul><p>Flattening immediately to one row loses the migration mechanics. Treating both rows as independent swaps doubles user activity and transaction-level fees.</p><h2>Takeaway</h2><p>This transaction is not a regular PumpFun buy followed by a PumpSwap trade. It is one <code>buy_exact_quote_in_v3</code> instruction with two internal economic phases:</p><ol><li><p>buy the remaining bonding-curve inventory;</p></li><li><p>buy additional tokens from the reserves destined for the future PumpSwap pool.</p></li></ol><p>The <code>is_post_trade</code> field is the key to reading it. Sum the two rows for the user’s total buy, keep them separate for reserve accounting, and carry the post-trade reserve state into the migration. When the pool appears, compare PumpSwap’s <strong>effective</strong> quote reserves—raw vault plus <code>virtual_quote_reserves</code>—with the migration output.</p><p>That is how the curve, the synthetic pool trade, and the first real PumpSwap state reconcile without a gap.</p><h2>Sources</h2><p>[1] https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/639b87fb45c3adeffa6f7c4a1bf0cb85e392bf22/docs/SYNTHETIC_MIGRATION.md — Pump synthetic migration [2] https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/639b87fb45c3adeffa6f7c4a1bf0cb85e392bf22/idl/pump.json — Pump program IDL</p><div><hr></div><p><em>OnChain Divers provides decoded Solana data: launch, swap and migration data for researchers building lifecycle-aware market models.
