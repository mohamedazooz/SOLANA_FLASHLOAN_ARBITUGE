# Raydium V4: cheaper direct swaps, more expensive aggregate flow

- **Date**: 2026-08-09
- **Original URL**: [https://onchaindivers.substack.com/p/raydium-v4-cheaper-direct-swaps-more](https://onchaindivers.substack.com/p/raydium-v4-cheaper-direct-swaps-more)
- **Description**: Raydium upgraded AMM V4 at Solana slot 434,518,095 on July 22, 2026.

---

<p>Raydium upgraded AMM V4 at Solana slot <strong>434,518,095</strong> on July 22, 2026.</p><p>Across adjacent 24-hour windows, aggregate median compute consumption rose from <strong>62,260 to 75,904 CU (+21.9%)</strong>; p90 increased <strong>26.8%</strong>.</p><p>Direct calls moved the other way: median fell from <strong>34,704 to 29,035 CU (−16.3%)</strong>, and p90 dropped <strong>42.2%</strong>. Their share declined from <strong>57.5% to 36.6%</strong>.
