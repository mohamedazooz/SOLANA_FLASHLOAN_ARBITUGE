Anyone can deposit USDC and receive equal amounts of both outcome tokens.</p><h3><strong>Merge Operation</strong></h3><p>Convert both outcome tokens back to USDC:</p><pre><code><code>1 Mavericks + 1 Nuggets → 1 USDC</code></code></pre><p>This is the reverse operation, also at a 1:1 ratio.</p><h3><strong>The Power of Symmetry</strong></h3><p>Because Mavericks + Nuggets = 1 USDC, the prices are
